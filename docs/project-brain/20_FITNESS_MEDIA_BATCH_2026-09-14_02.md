# MYPA Fitness Media Discovery Batch 02

Date: 2026-09-14
Status: RESEARCHED / RIGHTS-GATED

## Newly validated source families

- Shutterstock — commercial video licenses include app use; Enhanced video broadens audience/distribution coverage. Editorial assets are not interchangeable with commercial assets. See exact license before acquisition.
- Storyblocks — royalty-free licensing with commercial plans; license scope depends on plan and asset. Useful for supporting fitness/lifestyle footage rather than the canonical exercise-demo layer.
- Motion Array — most downloadable assets are cleared for commercial use, but raw asset distribution is prohibited and editorial assets are excluded. Candidate source for supporting media, not automatic exercise-demo approval.
- Depositphotos — royalty-free fitness/workout footage with Standard License commercial use advertised for websites/apps; exact clip license remains required.
- RepDB — 601-exercise free dataset, with original flat WebP illustrations, free commercial in-app use with visible attribution and no standalone dataset redistribution.
- Vital Animations — current terms explicitly state commercial licensing, self-hosting and embedding in commercial iOS/Android fitness apps; Complete Collection advertises 1,500+ animations covering gym, home, yoga, Pilates, office, women workouts and mobility/stretching.
- MoveKit — 400+ commercial exercise animations and a 412-animation Complete Pack; commercial app use allowed, raw animation redistribution prohibited.
- Your Move — 1,107+ exercise videos across gym, yoga, stretching, cardio and Pilates; paid API/app embedding options and separate self-hosting terms. Free 25-video set is commercial-use but not standalone redistribution.
- ExerciseVideoAPI — 290 filmed HD MP4 demonstrations with advertised perpetual commercial white-label license and self-hosting/download delivery.
- WorkoutDB — ~873 exercises with demo video; Pro/Scale plans advertise commercial in-product media/data use.
- Funxtion FXCONTENT — 3,500+ exercises, 1,000+ workouts, 650+ virtual classes; REST API/SDK and white-label delivery.
- Hyperhuman — licensed premium exercise clips/classes, yoga/Pilates/strength/HIIT/mobility/recovery; exact provider and export rights vary by agreement.

## High-value finding

Vital Animations and Your Move are unusually close to the 1,500-exercise target in catalog breadth. ExerciseVideoAPI is smaller but has explicit download/self-host/white-label language. These should be evaluated as procurement candidates before spending effort on hundreds of fragmented stock sources.

## Safety / rights rule

No source in this batch becomes production-approved merely because a website says "commercial". The intended MYPA model includes storing exercise demonstrations in the app and potentially in MYPA-controlled storage/CDN, so the exact license must explicitly support the needed delivery model. For stock platforms, asset-level model/property/editorial restrictions must be checked.

## Repository effect

Source inventory `data/fitness-media-source-inventory.seed.json` now tracks these source families and current rights status. Discovery count is **47 source records** as of this batch. The target remains 1,000 distinct source records; duplicates and multiple URLs under the same provider are not allowed to inflate the count.
